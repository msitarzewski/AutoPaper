Keyboard shortcuts

AutoPaper’s shortcuts work only in its own windows and menus. It never claims a key combination system-wide, so it can’t collide with anything you already use. Each app follows its own platform’s conventions, so the keys differ between them.

These are the shortcuts in AutoPaper 0.1.0.

Adding your own

macOS lets you give a shortcut to any command in an app’s menus: open System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts… → App Shortcuts, click +, choose AutoPaper, type the menu command’s name exactly (for example Pause New Wallpapers), and press the keys you want. This works for the commands in the menus above, while AutoPaper is the active app; it doesn’t reach the menu bar menu. AutoPaper’s actions in the Shortcuts app (see the Reference) can have keyboard shortcuts of their own too.

The notification-area icon

Press Win B to move focus to the notification area (a Windows shortcut), and the arrow keys to reach AutoPaper’s icon; it may be among the hidden icons. Then:

KeysWhat it does
Enter or SpaceShow AutoPaper
Shift F10 or the Menu keyAutoPaper’s menu: Like, Dislike, Mood, New wallpaper now, Pause new wallpapers, Restore my wallpaper, Show AutoPaper, Settings, Quit AutoPaper

In dialogs that ask before deleting or clearing something, the safe button (Cancel or Close) is the default, so Enter never deletes by accident.

On every system

  • Pause, Like and Dislike have no shortcut of their own; they’re in the menu bar or notification-area menu, the window, and (on Windows and Linux) the notification for each new wallpaper.
  • Each app is meant to work entirely from the keyboard, with Tab and the system’s usual keys, including reordering moods and keywords and every action on a past wallpaper. Where something doesn’t, that’s a bug: please open an issue.
